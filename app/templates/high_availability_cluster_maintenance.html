Safe ways forward

  1. Preferred: bring {{ dhcp_handover.source.display_name }} back on the network with its agent running. Its boot guard keeps DHCP off after a restart. When its agent reports, Kaya verifies that DHCP is stopped and hands DHCP over automatically.
  2. If {{ dhcp_handover.source.display_name }} cannot be brought back: an independent fencing provider (a power controller, a managed switch port or a hypervisor) must confirm that it is off or isolated. Kaya then promotes DHCP on {{ dhcp_handover.target.display_name }} from the last staged lease snapshot; leases issued after that snapshot may be missing. No fencing provider is configured for this cluster.

A typed confirmation cannot fence a node: the node may be switched back on or reconnected, or the wrong machine may have been checked. You can record what you physically checked for the audit trail, but Kaya will not start DHCP because of it.
